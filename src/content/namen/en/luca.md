## Meaning and origin

**Luca** goes back to the Latin name *Lucas*. It is usually linked to *lux*, “light”, and read
as *“bringer of light”*. Luca is a form of Luke and Lucas and is given in many countries.

## Variants

- **English:** Luke, Lucas
- **German:** Lukas, Lucas
- **Spanish:** Lucas
- **French:** Luc, Lucas
- **Dutch:** Lucas, Luuk

## Good to know

Luca is short, easy to spell and pronounced the same way in many languages. In a few countries
it is also used as a girl’s name, but it is mainly given to boys.
