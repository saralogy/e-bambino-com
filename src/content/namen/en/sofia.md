## Meaning and origin

**Sofia** comes from the Greek word *σοφία* (sophía), meaning *“wisdom”*. The name is used
across Europe and beyond, and has been among the popular girls’ names in many countries for years.

## Variants

- **English:** Sophia, Sophie
- **German:** Sophia, Sophie, Sofie
- **Spanish:** Sofía
- **French:** Sophie
- **Scandinavian:** Sofie, Sofia

## Good to know

Sofia and Sophia are pronounced the same way; the spelling with *f* is shorter and common in
many languages.
