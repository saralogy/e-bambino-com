A stroller is one of the first big purchases for a new baby, and the choice depends mostly on how you will use it.

## Do I need a newborn-friendly stroller?

If your baby will ride from the first weeks, choose a seat or bassinet that reclines fully. A newborn needs support for the head and back, so check the manufacturer's age guidance before you buy.

## Full-size or lightweight: which one first?

Many parents start with one full-size stroller for daily use and add a lightweight model later for travel. If you use only one stroller, look for a balance between fold, weight and newborn seating.
