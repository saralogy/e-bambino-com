## At a glance

- **When to pack:** start about one week before you leave
- **Time needed:** about 2 to 3 hours
- **Tip:** check your accommodation for a crib and high chair before you pack your own

## Documents

- [ ] Passports or ID cards for everyone, including your baby
- [ ] Health insurance cards and travel insurance details
- [ ] Booking confirmations for flights, trains, accommodation and rental cars
- [ ] Your child’s vaccination record
- [ ] Emergency contacts and your pediatrician’s number

## For your baby or toddler

- [ ] Diapers: the daily number for each day, plus a buffer
- [ ] Wipes and diaper cream
- [ ] Bodysuits, sleepers and socks
- [ ] Sleep sack
- [ ] Pacifier and a spare, if your child uses one
- [ ] Bottles and formula, or a nursing cover if you breastfeed
- [ ] Baby food and snacks
- [ ] Sippy cup
- [ ] Sunscreen for children and a sun hat
- [ ] Favorite toy or comforter
- [ ] Baby carrier and, if needed, a travel stroller

## Clothes for everyone

- [ ] Swimwear
- [ ] Sunglasses and hats
- [ ] Shoes and sandals
- [ ] T-shirts and shorts
- [ ] Sweater or jacket for cool evenings
- [ ] Pajamas
- [ ] Underwear and socks
- [ ] Quick-dry towels

## First aid kit

- [ ] Fever reducer suitable for your child’s age and weight, as advised by your pediatrician
- [ ] Thermometer
- [ ] Bandages and wound cream
- [ ] Disinfectant
- [ ] Oral rehydration salts
- [ ] Insect repellent suitable for children
- [ ] After-sun lotion
- [ ] Any medicines your child takes regularly

## Tech

- [ ] Phones and chargers
- [ ] Power bank
- [ ] Travel adapter, if you go abroad
- [ ] Downloaded films, music or audiobooks for the journey

## Tips for family travel

1. **Book early:** family rooms and seats together go fast
2. **Plan for delays:** travel with children rarely runs to schedule
3. **Check the accommodation:** crib, high chair, a children’s pool
4. **Pack the first aid kit in your hand luggage**
5. **Plan breaks:** don’t try to do too much in one day
