## At a glance

- **When to pack:** from about week 34 to 36 of pregnancy
- **Time needed:** about 1 to 2 hours
- **Tip:** ask your hospital what they provide, such as diapers, pads or baby clothes

## Documents

- [ ] ID card or passport
- [ ] Health insurance card or insurance details
- [ ] Pregnancy record or maternity notes
- [ ] Birth plan, if you have one
- [ ] Doctor’s letters for any medical conditions
- [ ] Marriage certificate or proof of paternity, if your country needs it to register the birth
- [ ] Pen and a few notes with important phone numbers

## For you

- [ ] Comfortable nightwear that opens at the front
- [ ] Robe and slippers or warm socks
- [ ] Nursing bras or soft bras
- [ ] Breast pads
- [ ] Maternity pads
- [ ] Comfortable, high-waisted underwear or disposable underwear
- [ ] Toiletries: toothbrush, toothpaste, shampoo, shower gel, deodorant
- [ ] Hairbrush and hair ties
- [ ] Lip balm
- [ ] Towel, if the hospital does not provide one
- [ ] Loose clothes for the trip home
- [ ] Nursing pillow, if you want one

## For your baby

- [ ] 2 to 3 bodysuits in size 50/56
- [ ] 2 to 3 footed sleepers in size 50/56
- [ ] Hat and socks
- [ ] Newborn diapers, if the hospital does not provide them
- [ ] A warm outfit or snowsuit for the way home, depending on the season
- [ ] Blanket for the trip home
- [ ] Infant car seat, installed in the car before the birth

## Other things

- [ ] Phone and charger with a long cable
- [ ] Power bank
- [ ] Headphones
- [ ] Snacks and a water bottle
- [ ] Book, e-reader or a playlist
- [ ] Some cash or a card for vending machines and parking

## Packing tips

1. **Pack early:** have the bag ready by week 36
2. **Keep it in one place:** by the door or in the car
3. **Keep it light:** a bag you can carry yourself
4. **Tell your birth partner:** they should know what is in it and where
5. **Stay flexible:** hospitals provide more than you might think

## What you can leave at home

- Too many clothes: you will mostly wear nightwear
- Expensive jewelry and valuables
- Large amounts of snacks, as hospitals provide meals
