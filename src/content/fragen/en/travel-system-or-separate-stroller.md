Shopping for a stroller and car seat often means choosing between a travel system and buying the two separately. This page covers fit, price, and flexibility. Safety judgments are left to the manufacturer's manuals and the official CPSC recall lists.

## Which pieces does a travel system include?
Check the packaging and manual for the exact stroller and car seat pieces included, and the models they are made for. Do not assume that two bundles with similar names include the same parts.

## Is a travel system cheaper than buying the parts separately?
This page does not list prices, so compare them yourself: the answer depends on the exact models and the retailer. Compare the bundle with the separate parts for the same models, on the same day, including any adapter you would need.

## How do you check that a car seat fits a stroller?
Start with the model numbers printed on the stroller leg and the car seat base, and check the manufacturer's compatibility list for those exact models. The CPSC has recalled stroller car seat adapters because they could crack or fail to attach, and recalled Combi Shuttle travel systems because the car seat could disengage from the stroller frame. Check both the stroller and the car seat model on the CPSC recall list before you buy. If you are also weighing stroller types, see [umbrella or full-size stroller](/on-the-go/umbrella-stroller-or-full-size/).

## When is a separate stroller and car seat the better choice?
A separate setup suits you best when the bundled models do not match your car or your stroller needs, or when you want to choose each part for its own strengths. Check that each part's stated age range fits your plans before you commit.

| Factor | Travel system | Separate stroller and car seat |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Compare the bundle with the parts for the exact models, on the same day | Same comparison, plus any adapter you need |
| Fit with your car | Check the car seat base in your car before you buy | Same check, for the exact car seat you choose |
| Stroller fit | Sold as a set; still confirm the model numbers | Confirm the model numbers on the stroller and car seat |
| Stated age range | Check each part's stated range | Check each part's stated range |
| Flexibility | Parts come as one bundle | You choose each piece for its own strengths |
