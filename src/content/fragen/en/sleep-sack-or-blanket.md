The key is matching the sleep sack’s warmth to the room, not the season.

## Why a sleep sack instead of a blanket?

A sleep sack cannot be kicked off or pulled over the face, so it keeps your baby warm without loose bedding. Safe-sleep guidance recommends nothing loose in the crib in the first year: no blankets, pillows, bumpers or soft toys.

## How do I tell if my baby is too warm?

Feel the back of the neck or the chest. Sweating, damp hair or hot, flushed skin means too warm. Cool hands and feet are normal and not a sign of being cold. If in doubt, use one layer less.

## Which TOG for which room temperature?

TOG is a measure of how warm a textile is. Use it together with what your baby wears underneath.

| Room temperature | Sleep sack TOG |
|---|---|
| Below 16 °C | 3.5 |
| 16 to 20 °C | 2.5 |
| 20 to 24 °C | 1.0 |
| Above 24 °C | 0.5 or none |

Always check the manufacturer’s guidance for clothing underneath.
