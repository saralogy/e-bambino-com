A newborn goes through 8 to 12 diapers a day. The number drops as your baby grows and the bladder holds more.

## How do I plan a month’s supply?

Multiply the daily number by 30. In the first months that is roughly 250 to 350 diapers; later it is less. Buy small packs of the newborn size at first, since many babies move to size 1 or 2 within a few weeks, and diapers that are too small leak.

## Are night diapers different?

Night diapers and the larger sizes absorb more, so your baby can sleep longer without leaks. They are not needed in the first weeks, when babies feed and get changed at night anyway.

## How do I know the size is right?

The waistband sits below the belly button without digging in, the leg cuffs close around the thighs without red marks, and there are no regular leaks. Leaks up the back or red marks on the legs usually mean it is time for the next size. Go by your baby’s weight on the pack rather than age.

| Age | Diapers per day | Per month (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| First weeks | 8 to 12 | 250 to 350 |
| 3 months | 7 to 10 | 210 to 300 |
| 6 months | 6 to 8 | 180 to 240 |
| 12 months | 5 to 7 | 150 to 210 |
| 24 months | 4 to 6 | 120 to 180 |
