Both have a clear strength. The question is less “which is better” and more “what do I need for this trip”.

## How do they differ?

A bassinet is a closed, flat bed on a stroller frame. Your baby lies fully supported and sheltered from wind and rain. A carrier or wrap holds your baby upright against your body, supports the head and back, and leaves your hands free.

## When is the bassinet better?

On long walks, in cold or wet weather, and when your baby should sleep for a long stretch. The downside is weight and space: stairs, buses and narrow shops are harder with a stroller.

## When is the carrier better?

In town, in shops and on public transport, and anywhere you stop and start often. Many babies settle quickly when carried close. A good hip belt moves the weight to your hips instead of your shoulders.

## How do I use a carrier safely?

Your baby should sit high enough to kiss the top of their head, with the face visible and not pressed into fabric or your body. Keep the chin off the chest so breathing stays free, and make sure the back is supported in its natural curve. Choose a carrier that fits your baby’s size and follow its instructions.

| Criterion | Bassinet | Carrier |
|---|---|---|
| Position | Lying flat | Upright against you |
| Weather protection | High | Low |
| Hands free | No | Yes |
| Head support | Built in | Carrier and your hold |
| Space needed | Large | Small |
