How long breast milk stays good depends on where you keep it and whether it has already been frozen and thawed. The times below follow CDC guidance, so you can use one set of rules at home, at work and on the road.

## How long can breast milk stay at room temperature?
Up to 4 hours, as long as the room is 77°F or colder. Once your baby starts drinking from a bottle, use the leftover milk within 2 hours after the feed ends, then throw it away. Do not save it for the next feeding.

## How long does breast milk keep in the refrigerator and freezer?
Fresh breast milk keeps up to 4 days in the refrigerator. In a freezer at 0°F or below, it is best used within about 6 months and is acceptable up to 12 months. Store milk at the back of the shelf rather than in the door, where the temperature changes each time it opens, and leave about one inch of space at the top of the container because milk expands as it freezes.

| Where the milk is | How long it keeps | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Room temperature (77°F or colder) | Up to 4 hours | Freshly expressed milk |
| Insulated cooler with frozen ice packs | Up to 24 hours | Handy for commutes and travel |
| Refrigerator | Up to 4 days | Not in the door |
| Freezer (0°F or below) | Best within about 6 months; acceptable up to 12 months | Leave about 1 inch at the top of the container |

## Can you refreeze breast milk after thawing?
No. Once breast milk has fully thawed, do not refreeze it. During an emergency such as a power outage, milk that is only partly thawed and still has ice crystals can go back in the freezer, while fully thawed milk that still feels cold should be refrigerated and used within a day. When in doubt, throw it out.

## How should you thaw and warm breast milk?
Thaw frozen milk in the refrigerator overnight, in a bowl of lukewarm water, or under lukewarm running water. Never thaw or heat breast milk in the microwave, because it heats unevenly and can create hot spots that burn your baby's mouth. Use milk thawed in the refrigerator within 24 hours of being fully thawed, and warmed milk within 2 hours.

## How do you keep pumped milk cold when you are out?
Pack it in an insulated cooler with frozen ice packs, where it keeps for up to 24 hours. Move it to a refrigerator or freezer as soon as you arrive. If you are traveling with your baby, add the cooler and ice packs to your [family travel checklist](/checklists/family-travel/).
