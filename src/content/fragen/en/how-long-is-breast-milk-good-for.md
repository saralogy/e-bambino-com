Breast milk storage times depend on where the milk is kept and whether it has been thawed. This page follows CDC guidance throughout, so you can use one consistent set of rules at home and when you are out. For feeding frequency, see [How often should a newborn breastfeed in 24 hours?](/breastfeeding/how-often-to-breastfeed-newborn/).

## How long can breast milk stay at room temperature?
Freshly expressed breast milk can stay at room temperature, 77°F or colder, for up to 4 hours. Milk left over from a feeding should be used within 2 hours after your baby finishes, then discarded. Do not store milk in the refrigerator or freezer door. If you are deciding how to pump, see [Manual or electric breast pump: which one do you need?](/breastfeeding/manual-or-electric-breast-pump/).

## How long does breast milk keep in the refrigerator and freezer?
Fresh breast milk keeps in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. In a freezer at 0°F or below, it is best used within about 6 months and is acceptable up to 12 months. Leave about one inch of space at the top of the container when you freeze it. The table below summarizes each storage type.

| Storage | How long | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Room temperature (77°F or colder) | Up to 4 hours | Fresh expressed milk |
| Refrigerator | Up to 4 days | Not in the door |
| Freezer (0°F or below) | Best used within about 6 months; acceptable up to 12 months | Leave about 1 inch of space at the top of the container |
| Thawed in the refrigerator | Use within 24 hours | Counted from when the milk is fully thawed |
| Warmed or left at room temperature | Use within 2 hours | Applies to warmed milk and milk left out |
| Insulated cooler with frozen ice packs | Up to 24 hours | Keep milk out of the refrigerator and freezer door |

## Can you refreeze breast milk after thawing?
No. Milk that has fully thawed must never be refrozen, according to CDC. In an emergency such as a power outage, partly thawed milk that still has ice crystals can be refrozen. Fully thawed milk that still feels cold should be refrigerated and used within a day, or discarded. When in doubt, throw it out.

## How should you thaw breast milk safely?
Thaw breast milk in the refrigerator overnight, in lukewarm water, or under lukewarm running water. Do not thaw or heat it in a microwave, because it can create hot spots. Use milk thawed in the refrigerator within 24 hours of being fully thawed, and use warmed milk within 2 hours.
