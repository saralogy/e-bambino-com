Screen advice for young children is not one global rule. This page sets out what the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) and the German AWMF guideline recommend, and where they do not agree.

## Is screen time recommended for babies under 18 months?
No. The AAP advises pediatricians to discourage screen media for children under 18 months, with video chatting as the exception. The German AWMF guideline goes further and recommends keeping children under 3 away from all passive and active screen use. Kindergesundheit-info, a German parent resource, likewise advises that babies and toddlers under three should not watch television yet.

## How much screen time is reasonable for ages 2 to 5?
For children aged 2 to 5, the AAP advises no more than 1 hour of digital media per day, with co-viewing recommended, meaning a parent watches with the child. Its 2025 policy statement takes a looser view of fixed limits and does not replace these 2016 age bands. The German AWMF guideline recommends screen-free use until age three, and the sources reviewed here did not confirm a German limit for ages 3 to 5.

| Age | Recommended limit | What a parent can do |
|---|---|---|
| Under 18 months | AAP: avoid screen media except video chatting. AWMF: keep away from all screen use. | Use video chat for family contact. Keep bedrooms, mealtimes and parent-child playtime screen-free. |
| 18 to 24 months | AAP: if you introduce media, choose high-quality programs or apps and use them with your child; avoid solo use. AWMF: screen-free until age three. | Watch or play together. The two guidelines do not agree at this age. |
| 2 to 5 years | AAP: no more than 1 hour per day, with co-viewing. AWMF: screen-free until age three; a German limit for ages 3 to 5 is not confirmed here. | Watch together and keep to the daily limit. Keep meals and bedrooms screen-free. |
| 5 and older | Not set in the sources used. The AAP's 2025 statement says time may vary by family, with needs and school nights versus weekends considered. | Agree on routines for school nights and weekends. Keep meals and bedrooms screen-free. |

## Is video chatting with family different from TV or apps?
Yes, for children under 18 months. The AAP names video chatting as the one screen use it does not discourage. The German sources reviewed here do not confirm whether video calls are allowed under age three, so check with your pediatrician.

## What can parents do instead of screens during the day?
Start with the places the AAP names: keep bedrooms, mealtimes and parent-child playtime screen-free. The sources used for this page do not describe specific alternative activities, so this section does not list any.
