If your breasts leak between feeds, a nursing pad can help keep your clothes dry. This page covers when pads help and which kinds to avoid.

## Do you need nursing pads if your breasts leak?
Nursing pads help if your breasts leak a lot between feedings, because they absorb milk that leaks between feeds. Disposable and washable pads can both be slipped inside the bra cup.

## How do you use nursing pads?
Slip the pad inside the bra cup to catch milk that leaks between feedings. Disposable and washable pads both work this way. The sources we checked do not rank disposable pads above washable ones, so choose by what suits you.

## Which nursing pads should you avoid?
Avoid pads with plastic liners, because they block air flow around the nipples and can irritate them. Before you buy, check the following.

- [ ] The pad goes inside the bra cup, as disposable and washable types both do.
- [ ] The pad has no plastic liner that blocks air flow around the nipples.
- [ ] You need a pad because your breasts leak a lot between feedings.
