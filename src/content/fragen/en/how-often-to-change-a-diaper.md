Eight to twelve changes a day is typical in the first weeks. The number drops as your baby feeds less often and the bladder holds more. The state of the diaper matters more than the count.

## How do I know the diaper is full?

Most disposable diapers have a wetness indicator stripe that changes color. Otherwise, a heavy, squishy diaper is wet. Change after every poop, because stool on the skin is the main cause of diaper rash.

## How do I clean the skin?

Warm water and a soft cloth are enough for most changes. If you use wipes, choose fragrance-free ones. Pat the skin dry, especially in the folds, and let it air for a moment before closing the new diaper.

## How do I prevent diaper rash?

Change often, clean gently and keep the skin dry. A thin layer of barrier cream with zinc oxide protects irritated skin. Some diaper-free time helps red skin recover. If the rash is severe, has blisters or open spots, or does not improve within a few days, see your pediatrician.

| Age | Changes per day (guide) |
