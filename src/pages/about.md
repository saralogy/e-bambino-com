# About e-bambino.com

**e-bambino.com** is the premium guide for baby and mom: clear answers, honest product
comparisons and checklists for pregnancy and the first years, all in one place.

## Why we exist

Expecting and new parents face hundreds of questions and buying decisions, often at the same
time and usually for the first time. We bring together what is otherwise spread across twenty
browser tabs: a clear answer, help with the decision, and a list you can tick off.

## What makes us different

- **The answer comes first.** Every question is answered in one paragraph before we go into detail.
- **Checklists you can tick off.** The hospital bag is not an article, it is a list you print and work through.
- **Honest comparisons.** We say what a product suits and what it does not. Placements are not for sale.
- **Checked by people.** Health and safety topics are published only after review by a named expert.

## How we work

Every page follows the same steps before it goes live:

1. **Research** against a fixed list of sources (see [Sources](/sources/))
2. **Writing** in a fixed page template
3. **Fact check**: every figure against its original source
4. **Expert review** for health and safety topics, by a named professional
5. **Sign-off** by a human editor, always, without exception

All content on this site is written and checked by people, which is why the exemption for
human-edited content under **EU AI Act Art. 50** applies.

## Our sources

We only cite reliable, publicly available sources, among them:

- **WHO**: World Health Organization
- **AWMF**: guidelines of the German scientific medical societies
- **RKI**: Robert Koch Institute
- National statistics offices in Germany, Austria and Switzerland

The full list is on the [Sources](/sources/) page.

## Transparency

We do not sell data, we do not show advertising without labeling it, and we do not accept
payment for articles. If a page is out of date, tell us and we will correct it.

**Contact:** kontakt@e-bambino.com, for corrections, sources and press inquiries.
