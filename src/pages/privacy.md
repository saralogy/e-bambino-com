# Privacy policy

The controller for data processing on this website is e-bambino, Hamburg, Germany,
info@e-bambino.com. The binding version is the [German privacy policy](/de/datenschutz/).

## 1. Server log files

When you visit this website, the hosting service automatically records information your
browser transmits:

- The page visited and the date and time of access
- The amount of data transferred in bytes
- The referrer URL and IP address (shortened or anonymized by Cloudflare)

**Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in operating the website
securely and reliably.

## 2. Hosting

This website is delivered via **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare Inc., USA). A data processing
agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Cloudflare.

> **Note:** the Cloudflare plan determines which analytics products are active. If
> Web Analytics is enabled, additional consent under § 25 TDDDG is required.

## 3. Cookies

This website uses **no tracking or marketing cookies**. Technically necessary cookies are only
set where there is a legal basis for them (for example session handling in the CMS).

## 4. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the information it contains to
handle your request.

- **Legal basis:** Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR
- **Retention:** until your request has been handled, then according to statutory retention periods

## 5. External content

Our site does not embed external fonts, trackers or social media widgets. All content is
delivered from our own servers.

## 6. AI-generated content (EU AI Act Art. 50)

All editorial content on this website is **written by human authors and checked before
publication**. It is not AI-generated or AI-manipulated content within the meaning of Art. 50
of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act).

## 7. Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

- **Access** the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- **Rectification** (Art. 16 GDPR)
- **Erasure** (Art. 17 GDPR)
- **Restriction of processing** (Art. 18 GDPR)
- **Data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR)
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)

An informal message to info@e-bambino.com is enough.

## 8. Right to complain

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in
particular the authority responsible for us.
