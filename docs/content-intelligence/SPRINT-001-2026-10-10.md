## Executive decision

The first sprint confirms a meaningful content-format gap: the site has short answer pages and a few checklists, but the roadmap itself calls out a missing buying-guide template and three empty English hubs (Bath & care, Breastfeeding, Safety). Competitors frequently combine selection criteria with a decision framework, comparison, checklist or product-testing methodology. e-bambino should compete by helping parents decide, measure, calculate or complete a task—not by copying ranked product lists.

## Category-by-category findings

### Clothing
**Observed:** Existing EN pages include newborn size, when to buy the next size, sleeper vs bodysuit, and washing baby clothes. C&A's guide emphasizes body length rather than age and explains brand size variation: https://www.c-and-a.com/eu/en/shop/baby-clothes-sizes. The AAP's layette page gives a starter list but is older and should not be copied as a universal quantity prescription: https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/prenatal/decisions-to-make/Pages/Choosing-a-Layette.aspx.
**Gap hypothesis:** A size-and-wardrobe planner that reduces overbuying adds more value than another “baby clothing sizes by age” page. The page agent must check overlap and avoid duplicating `newborn-clothing-size` or `when-to-buy-the-next-size`.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-clothing-size-wardrobe-planner.md`.

### On the go
**Observed:** Current EN coverage includes `when-can-a-baby-use-a-stroller`, `baby-carrier-or-bassinet`, and a forward-facing car-seat question. Which? structures pushchair advice around tested products, categories, cost and newborn buying advice: https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/pushchairs/article/most-popular-pushchairs-to-buy-a37Yk9K2bU3z. BabyGearLab compares stroller categories and explains its testing and affiliate disclosure: https://www.babygearlab.com/topics/getting-around/best-stroller.
**Gap hypothesis:** A constraint-first guide—home measurements, transit, terrain, trunk/lift and folding—can be more actionable and independent than “best stroller” lists. The roadmap already identifies a stroller buying guide as a high-priority gap.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-on-the-go-stroller-buying-guide.md`.

### Diapering
**Observed:** Existing EN pages cover daily quantity, changing frequency and cloth vs disposable. A current competitor provides cost estimates for cloth, disposable and service options: https://usefulhq.com/cloth-vs-disposable-diapers/. Its figures are not authoritative and should not be copied without independent price inputs.
**Gap hypothesis:** An interactive total-cost calculator could distinguish the site from a static comparison, particularly by including upfront cloth costs, laundry, service fees, local unit prices and reuse across children.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-diapering-total-cost-calculator.md`.

### Feeding
**Observed:** Existing EN coverage has `high-chair-age`; it does not appear to include a full selection guide. Consumer Reports evaluates chair type, safety features, folding/storage, footrests and ease of cleaning: https://www.consumerreports.org/babies-kids/high-chairs/buying-guide/.
**Gap hypothesis:** Parents need to match features to the actual table, space, cleaning routine and expected use period. Use a neutral comparison worksheet, not a fabricated ranking or unperformed product test.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-feeding-high-chair-buying-guide.md`.

### Bath & care
**Observed:** The English hub has no question pages in the current repository inventory. NHS guidance emphasizes preparing everything before bathing, not leaving a baby alone, and keeping the setup simple: https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/washing-and-bathing-your-baby/.
**Gap hypothesis:** A minimum-kit workflow can answer “what do I actually need?” and avoid an inflated shopping list. The page must not imply that a particular bath gadget is required.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-bath-care-first-bath-checklist.md`.

### Breastfeeding
**Observed:** The English hub has no question pages in the current inventory. CDC guidance covers storage, transport, labeling, leftover milk and equipment hygiene: https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding/breast-milk-preparation-and-storage/handling-breastmilk.html and https://www.cdc.gov/hygiene/about/about-breast-pump-hygiene.html.
**Gap hypothesis:** One end-to-end workflow can reduce fragmentation between storage, daycare handoff and cleaning. The CDC guidance is US-based; the writer must state jurisdiction and check German/EU recommendations rather than presenting it as universal.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-breastfeeding-pumping-storage-workflow.md`.

### Nursery
**Observed:** Existing EN pages include crib lifespan, small-apartment nursery, and sleep sack vs blanket; there is one general sleep guide. CPSC details crib-mattress fit, dimensions and safety hazards: https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Crib-Mattresses; safe-sleep basics: https://www.cpsc.gov/SafeSleep.
**Gap hypothesis:** A fit/inspection checklist is more distinct than another broad nursery article. Used-product checks must never be framed as proving an item is safe.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-nursery-crib-mattress-checklist.md`.

### Play
**Observed:** Existing EN pages cover motor-skill toys, first books and play mats. Parents publishes age-banded toy roundups: https://www.parents.com/parents-best-toys-awards-2026-baby-12124605; CPSC publishes age-related toy guidance: https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/2_0.pdf.
**Gap hypothesis:** A toy-rotation and borrow/buy/skip planner can add cost-conscious utility without competing on product awards. Age labels and safety warnings must be distinguished from developmental claims.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-play-toy-rotation-planner.md`.

### Safety
**Observed:** German EN-equivalent content is sparse: English has no safety-hub question pages in the current inventory, while German has a few related pages (outlet safety, fall prevention, dangerous household items, changing-table falls). CPSC provides a free official checklist: https://www.cpsc.gov/safety-education/safety-guides/kids-and-babies/baby-safety-checklist; commercial/parenting sites provide room-by-room printable checklists, e.g. https://www.consumerreports.org/babyproofing/babyproofing-checklist-baby-safety-guide-a3304634900/.
**Gap hypothesis:** A severity-first checklist with mobility-stage re-checks can improve on a long undifferentiated list. German version must rely on relevant DE/EU authorities and avoid US-only standards unless explicitly labeled.
**Brief:** `inbox/2026-10-10-safety-room-by-room-checklist.md`.

## Technical SEO/GEO review

### Confirmed observations from code
1. **robots.txt has two `User-agent: *` groups and disallows `/_astro/`.** This conflicts with the project's own `AGENTS.md` to-do. Remove the duplicate wildcard group and do not block Astro asset paths without a specific verified reason. Keep the sitemap declaration.
2. **Potential duplicate Article structured data on question pages.** `src/views/Question.astro` emits an `@graph` containing Article and BreadcrumbList, while its Layout is also passed `type="article"`; `src/components/SEO.astro` emits a separate Article object for that layout. Confirm the final rendered HTML, then consolidate to one Article entity plus one BreadcrumbList. Avoid assuming that more JSON-LD is better.
3. **Good foundation already exists:** question pages have answer-first content, updated dates, a source link, conditional reviewer display, Article/BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, canonical/hreflang support in shared SEO and sibling links. Build on it rather than replacing templates.
4. **Source registry gap:** current keys include WHO, AWMF, RKI, kindergesundheit-info, Verbraucherzentrale, UNECE and CPSC. CDC is not currently a registered key. If CDC is used on the English breastfeeding page, add a named source to `src/views/Sources.astro` and the display map in `src/views/Question.astro` before referencing the key in content.
5. **No real search-performance dataset was inspected.** Search volume, ranking positions, click-through rate and actual AI citation frequency remain unknown. Connect Search Console/analytics when available; do not use competitor presence as a proxy for demand.
6. **Publication gate remains mandatory.** `src/lib/shipgate.ts` holds every YMYL page without a named reviewer and every page without a source key. Do not weaken it.

### Technical priority
- **P1:** fix wildcard robots group / `/_astro/` disallow; validate assets are crawlable as appropriate.
- **P1:** inspect rendered question page JSON-LD and consolidate duplicate Article entities if confirmed.
- **P1:** implement a buying-guide content type/template only after agreeing on the shared page architecture; use the stroller guide as the pilot.
- **P2:** add a lightweight English audit mirroring the existing German content quality checks.
- **P2:** create related-content links from question → buying guide → hub where the relationship is genuinely useful.
- **P2:** measure Search Console impressions/clicks and AI referral/citation observations only where a reliable source is available.
