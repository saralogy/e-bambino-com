## Recommendation
Build a stroller buying guide that begins with the family's real constraints—not product awards. The current site answers when a baby can use a stroller, but a parent still needs to choose a type and verify that it fits their home and travel.

## Existing-site overlap
Existing EN pages include `when-can-a-baby-use-a-stroller`, `baby-carrier-or-bassinet` and a car-seat orientation question. Keep the guide about choosing a stroller; link to those pages for age, carrier/bassinet and car-seat questions.

- Which? 2026 pushchair advice: https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/pushchairs/article/most-popular-pushchairs-to-buy-a37Yk9K2bU3z — categories, newborn purchase advice and price are visible in the page structure. Observed 2026-10-10.

These competitors use rankings/testing that e-bambino has not performed. Do not imitate their awards or claim products were tested.

## Differentiated utility
A constraint-first chooser:
- terrain and daily route
- stairs/elevator and storage location
- folded dimensions and car trunk
- public-transport use and weight to lift
- newborn configuration and compatible accessories
- cleaning, repairability and second-hand considerations
- realistic total cost of the complete configuration

Include a blank comparison matrix parents can fill with manufacturer specifications. Explain that compatibility must be confirmed in the exact model's manual.

## Suggested page outline
**Title:** How to Choose a Stroller for Your Home, Routes and Budget
**Meta:** Compare stroller types against your daily routes, storage space, transport needs and budget before choosing a model.
**H1:** Which stroller fits your everyday life?
- What are the main stroller types?
- What measurements should you take before buying?
- Which features matter for public transport, stairs or a small car?
- What does a travel system actually require?
- What should you check when buying second-hand?
- Which questions should you ask before paying?
