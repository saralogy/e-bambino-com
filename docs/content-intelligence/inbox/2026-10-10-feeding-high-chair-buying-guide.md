# Content opportunity: How to choose a high chair

- **Category:** Feeding
- **Priority:** P1
- **Decision:** Build a buying guide; link to existing age/readiness answer
- **Research date:** 2026-10-10
- **Confidence:** High for competitor coverage and current-site gap

## Recommendation
The existing `high-chair-age` page answers when a high chair may be appropriate, not which design fits a family's space, table, cleaning routine and expected years of use. Build the decision guide without claiming that e-bambino has tested products.

## Competitor evidence
- Consumer Reports buying guide: https://www.consumerreports.org/babies-kids/high-chairs/buying-guide/ — covers chair types, storage/folding, restraints, footrests, tray handling, cleanability and online-shopping risks. Updated 2 April 2026; observed 2026-10-10.
- Consumer Reports also describes a testing methodology for safety, ease of use and cleaning. e-bambino has not run such tests and must not present a comparable ranking.

## Differentiated utility
Create a parent-fillable comparison table with: footprint, folded storage, table compatibility, tray removal, harness adjustability, cleaning seams/straps, replacement parts, conversion lifespan, weight limit from manual, and total cost. Add a “measure your space first” checklist. Clearly separate manufacturer specs from editorial selection criteria.

## Suggested outline
**Title:** How to Choose a High Chair: Fit, Cleaning and Space
**Meta:** Compare high chairs by fit at your table, storage space, cleaning effort, adjustability and the manufacturer’s safety instructions.
**H1:** What should you look for when choosing a high chair?
- Which high-chair types exist?
- What should you measure at home?
- Which features make daily cleaning easier?
- What safety instructions must you verify in the manual?
- Is a convertible chair worth the extra cost?
