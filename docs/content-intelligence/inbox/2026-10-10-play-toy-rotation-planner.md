# Content opportunity: Toy rotation and borrow/buy/skip planner for the first year

- **Category:** Play
- **Priority:** P2
- **Decision:** Build a practical planner or checklist; no product-ranking page
- **Research date:** 2026-10-10
- **Confidence:** Medium — competitors and official safety guidance observed; demand not quantified

## Recommendation
Existing pages cover motor-skill toys, first books and play mats. Do not create another “best toys by age” list. Build a low-cost planning tool that helps parents rotate what they already own, choose simple play activities and distinguish optional purchases from safety requirements.

## Competitor evidence
- Parents’ baby toy awards: https://www.parents.com/parents-best-toys-awards-2026-baby-12124605 — age/development themed product recommendations with prices and purchase links. Observed 2026-10-10.
- CPSC toy age guidance: https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/2_0.pdf — gives age-related toy/play examples and cautions against unsuitable small objects. Observed 2026-10-10.
- Treat product awards as competitor format evidence only; e-bambino has not tested these products.

## Differentiated utility
- Weekly rotation sheet with “already own / borrow / buy only if useful”
- Simple interaction/activity ideas that do not require buying a product
- Manufacturer age warning and small-parts check
- Avoid unsupported developmental milestone promises or “boosts intelligence” claims

## Suggested outline
**Title:** Baby Toy Rotation: A Simple, Low-Cost Planner
**Meta:** Plan simple play activities and rotate toys you already have, with practical reminders about age labels and safety.
**H1:** How can you organize baby toys without buying too many?
