- **Category:** Clothing

- **Market:** English root first; DE version only after German sources and translation mapping are checked

## Recommendation
Build a combined size-planning and starter-wardrobe worksheet, not another generic age-to-size chart. Parents need to decide what to buy now, what to delay until birth measurements are known, and how to avoid overbuying the smallest size.

## Existing-site overlap
Existing EN pages: `newborn-clothing-size`, `when-to-buy-the-next-size`, `sleeper-or-bodysuit`, `how-to-wash-baby-clothes`. Do not duplicate these. The new guide should link to them and focus on a joined-up planning task.

## Competitor evidence
- C&A sizing guide: https://www.c-and-a.com/eu/en/shop/baby-clothes-sizes — emphasizes body length and variation between age labels and actual fit. Observed 2026-10-10.
- AAP HealthyChildren layette page: https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/prenatal/decisions-to-make/Pages/Choosing-a-Layette.aspx — offers a starter list and cautions that newborn sizes may be outgrown quickly. Observed 2026-10-10; dated content, so use as an example of format rather than a universal quantity source.

## Differentiated utility
- Size table based on measured length, with brand variation caveat.
- “Buy now / wait / borrow” checklist.
- Small starter-set planner with editable quantity ranges and a rationale for each item.
- Separate sleepwear from general clothing; safety claims about cords/fit must be sourced.

## Suggested page outline
**Title:** Baby Clothes Sizes and Starter Wardrobe: What to Buy First
**Meta:** Use your baby’s measured length to understand clothing sizes, then plan a small starter wardrobe without buying every size in advance.
**H1:** How do you choose baby clothes sizes and what do you need first?
- What does the EU size number mean?
- Why does age not reliably predict fit?
- What should you buy before birth—and what can wait?
- How many items do you need for laundry frequency and climate?
- What should you check on labels and fastenings?
