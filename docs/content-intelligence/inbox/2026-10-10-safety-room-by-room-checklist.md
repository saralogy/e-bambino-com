## Recommendation

## Competitor and official evidence

- Consumer Reports babyproofing guide: https://www.consumerreports.org/babyproofing/babyproofing-checklist-baby-safety-guide-a3304634900/ — room-by-room and age-by-age guidance plus product testing. Observed 2026-10-10.
- Checklist.com: https://checklist.com/baby-proofing-checklist — interactive checklist, printable/downloadable options and prioritization by hazard. Observed 2026-10-10.

Competitors show that checklists and staged tasks are common formats. Do not copy their wording, assume their medical/legal accuracy or claim e-bambino has tested safety products.

## Differentiated utility
- Separate “address immediately” high-severity hazards from room-by-room follow-up
- Map re-checks to rolling, crawling, pulling up and climbing
- Separate hazard mitigation from optional products; no default shopping cart
- DE/EU-specific links and recall sources for the German version
- Clear disclaimer that checklist completion does not replace supervision or product instructions

**Meta:** Review common home hazards room by room, prioritize urgent fixes and repeat checks when your child’s mobility changes.
