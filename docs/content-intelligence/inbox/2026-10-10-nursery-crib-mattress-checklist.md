- **Category:** Nursery
- **Priority:** P2
- **Decision:** Build a narrow safety checklist rather than another general nursery guide

## Recommendation
Existing EN pages cover crib lifespan and nursery planning. A narrow measurement/inspection checklist may fill a useful gap, but must not claim that an old or second-hand crib is safe based on a visual check alone.

## Evidence
- CPSC crib mattress FAQ: https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Crib-Mattresses — addresses fit, dimensions, thickness, compatibility and other hazards. Observed 2026-10-10.
- CPSC Safe Sleep: https://www.cpsc.gov/SafeSleep — advises a firm, flat, intended sleep surface with a fitted sheet and no loose bedding. Observed 2026-10-10.
- AAP safe-sleep summary: https://www.aap.org/en/patient-care/safe-sleep/. Observed 2026-10-10.

US standards and recommendations are not automatically equivalent to EU requirements. Find applicable European/German standards and consumer-safety guidance before DE publication.

## Differentiated utility
- “Measure before purchase” worksheet using the exact crib and mattress manuals
- Condition checks for damage, missing parts, missing labels and unknown product history
- Clear “stop and verify with manufacturer/authority” cases
- A reminder that a checklist is not certification and cannot prove an item safe
