# Category charter: Play
Own only the `play` hub (German taxonomy key `spielzeug`): books, toys, play mats, sensory/play equipment, age-stage choices, durability, storage and low-cost/no-buy activities.

Seek value-added ideas such as toy rotation planner, book-selection guide, age-stage activity cards, toy material/cleaning comparison and borrow/buy/skip guidance. Do not treat age labels as universal developmental rules; clearly separate manufacturer age warnings from editorial guidance.

Choking hazards, magnets, button batteries, cords and developmental/health claims require authoritative sources (e.g. official product-safety agencies and recognized health institutions). Do not invent test scores, certifications or expert review.

Use the standard brief and include current-page overlap, competitor URLs, parent intent, differentiating utility, citations, FAQ/question cluster, internal links, structured data/SEO/GEO recommendations and exact handoff for the page agent. Do not write production content.
